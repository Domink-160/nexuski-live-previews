The Client pays standard third-party platform hosting directly to Framer, with zero ongoing fees owed to Croft Web Studio.

  • 100% Ownership Transfer Guarantee: Upon regular cancellation of the Managed Care Plan after the initial 6-month term (or in the event Croft Web Studio ever discontinues managed hosting), Croft Web Studio transfers 100% ownership of the website project to the Client's own Framer account free of any transfer fee.
